# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Support**:
The Cadence staff who decide Refund Requests and confirm Refunds. Today that is every admin.
_Avoid_: The team, Team (a Team is a buyer of team licence seats)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. Support approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request, provided they have watched less than two hours and completed less than half of the course's lessons.
_Avoid_: Grace period, Cooling-off period

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support hasn't decided yet. The learner keeps the course while it is open.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support has accepted. Approving it leads to a Refund and immediately ends the learner's access to the course; any unclaimed team licence seats on the purchase are voided.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request Support has turned down, always with a note explaining why. It has no Refund, the learner keeps the course, and they may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund Support has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner. Nothing happens to a Refund after it is issued.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and Support may try again until the Refund is issued.
