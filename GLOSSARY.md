# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers who uses Cadence and the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, User

**Instructor**:
Someone who creates and owns courses.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author

**Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Support Agent, Staff, Reviewer, Team Admin (for a Team Manager)

**Team Manager**:
A Learner who manages the seats on a team purchase.
_Avoid_: Team Admin, Team Owner

**Seat**:
A place on a team purchase that one Learner can claim to get the course. Seats stop working once the team purchase's Refund is Issued.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence

### Learning

**Progress**:
A Learner's record of how far they've got through a course. It is never deleted by a Refund: it stays hidden while they have no access, and returns if they buy the course again.
_Avoid_: History, Completion (for the whole record)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once, and none can follow an approved one.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Learner can raise a Refund Request for a purchase right now. A Learner who isn't eligible is never offered the chance to ask.
_Avoid_: Refundable, Refund window (for the whole rule)

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Decided Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Processed, Closed, Resolved

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund. Approval is final and can't be undone.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, always with a reason the Learner can see. It has no Refund, and the Learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet. The Learner keeps access to the course.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the Learner. This is the moment the Learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the Learner keeps access until an Admin retries it and it is Issued.
